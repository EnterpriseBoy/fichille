About Draíodóirí Fichille

Draíodóirí Fichille is a community built for chess lovers of all ages and skill levels. We focus on strategy, creativity, and the joy of learning through play.

Whether you're a beginner discovering the basics or an advanced player sharpening your tactical edge, our club provides coaching, events, and a welcoming environment.

Our Mission

To inspire strategic thinking, encourage curiosity, and build a strong chess community across Blackrock and beyond.

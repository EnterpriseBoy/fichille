About Chess Wizards

Chess Wizards is a community created for chess players of all ages and skill levels. We focus on strategy, creativity, and the enjoyment that comes from learning through play.

Whether you are a beginner learning the basics, or an advanced player looking to improve your tactical skills, our club provides training, events, and a friendly and welcoming environment.

Our Mission

Our mission is to encourage strategic thinking, foster curiosity, and build a strong chess community in Rockfield and beyond.

Chess Pieces

There are six types of pieces in a game of chess. Each piece moves in a different way and plays an important role in the game.

Piece
Description
Pronunciation
♔
The King

The king is the most important piece in the game. It can move one square in any direction. The king must always be protected.

♕
The Queen

The queen is the most powerful piece. It can move up, down, left, right, and diagonally across the board.

♖
The Rook

The rook can move in a straight line up, down, left, or right. It is very effective when there are open lines on the board.

♗
The Bishop

The bishop moves diagonally. Each bishop stays on the same colour square throughout the game.

♘
The Knight

The knight moves in an L-shape: two squares in one direction and one square to the side. It is the only piece that can jump over other pieces.

♙
The Pawn

The pawn normally moves forward, but captures another piece diagonally. On its first move, it can move two squares forward.

Beginner Tip

Try to control the centre of the board and develop your pieces early in the game. Don't forget to keep your king safe!
